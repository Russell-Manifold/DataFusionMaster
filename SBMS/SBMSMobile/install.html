Install Our Mobile App

Android/Chrome:

  1. Open Chrome browser on your Android device
  2. Navigate to our main page
  3. Tap the menu (3 dots) in top right
  4. Select "Add to Home screen"
  5. Confirm the installation

iPhone/Safari:

  1. Open Safari browser on your iPhone
  2. Go to our main page
  3. Tap the share button (square with arrow)
  4. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen"
  5. Tap "Add" to confirm

Once installed, the app will open fullscreen without the browser bar!
